The Update

The Update is the lifeblood of West Peek — a safe space to share wins, big or small, ask for help, and talk through what's actually going on with what you're building. From what you share, we make the introductions, curate the workshops, and extend the dinner invites that fit where you are right now. We want you to feel less lonely as you build. It is not our independence but our interconnectedness that gets us there.

Cadence: quarterly. A fast baseline (share a win or a shout-out) takes a minute; an optional deeper layer (what you're stuck on, who you want to meet) unlocks curated introductions.
