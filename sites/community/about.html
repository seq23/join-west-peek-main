The history of West Peek

West Peek began on Sunday, March 22, 2020, and it grew into everything the community is today.

Origin

March 22, 2020

West Peek started in 2020, during the global pandemic. On Sunday, March 22, 2020, 5,000 people virtually gathered for QuarantineCon — a digital conference held the first weekend of the global pandemic. We listened to medical professionals, investors, and creatives, and left inspired. We saw then the power of community while building your dreams, and West Peek is the continuation of that.

Founding Team

Who built it

Scooter Taylor

Scooter Taylor is a co-founder of West Peek. He attended Morehouse College, where his studies focused on Marketing. Since his undergraduate days, his work has been at the intersection of technology and culture, launching venture-backed technology companies with investors that included Comcast Ventures, Morgan Stanley, and Y Combinator. He is currently the head of marketing at All Def, a media platform that reaches over 30 million people monthly. Taylor is guided by his passion for building online communities and giving others a platform. In March 2020, he launched QuarantineCon, the digital gathering that became West Peek.

Tre’von Hill

Tre’von Hill is a co-founder of West Peek. He attended Morehouse College, where he majored in Psychology. He has taken technical and synthesizing skills learned in the lab into the world of venture. With a focus on bridging the technology and economic equity gap within communities of color, Hill works with founders, business professionals, local government leaders, and investors on projects, products, and campaigns focused on the triple bottom line. He has a passion for connecting people with purpose by providing the resources and opportunities they need to execute their ideas.
