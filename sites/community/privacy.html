Privacy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who we are

This site is run by West Peek. Questions or requests about your information: os@joinwestpeek.com.

What you give us

When you fill in a form we receive what you type (such as your name, email, company and message).

We use it to reply to you, and we add you to the West Peek network list so we can keep in touch. We do not sell it.

What we measure

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and buttons people use. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your browser’s details and an approximate location. We send it the page address with personal details removed from the link. Ad personalisation and Google signals are turned off. Google explains how it uses this information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Who else handles it

Our site host and email services (Cloudflare, Resend) and Google (for our records) process it on our behalf.

Your choices

Email us to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to come off our list. You can also block or clear cookies in your browser.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
