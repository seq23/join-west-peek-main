Join the West Peek community

More than 5,000 founders, operators, investors, creatives and builders. Free to join, and we only email when there is something real to say.

The short answer

Joining is free. You get the newsletter, invitations to events, and podcast updates. Nothing else, and no third party gets your address.

Who this is for

  • FoundersAt any stage, including the stage before it is obvious there is a company.
  • OperatorsThe people who actually build and run things inside companies.
  • InvestorsParticularly ones who like meeting founders before a round is a round.
  • Creatives and buildersBecause the interesting introductions are rarely between two people with the same job title.

What you actually get

  • The newsletterReal updates, not a content schedule.
  • Event invitationsSmall events, which is the point. A room where you can meet everybody beats a room where you can meet nobody.
  • Podcast dropsCurrently published on Scooter Taylor's YouTube channel. More on the podcast page.

What you will not get

A daily send. A drip sequence. Your email address handed to anyone else. If we have nothing worth saying, we do not send anything — which is why the list moves slowly and why the open rates are worth having.

Join the list

If the form fails to send, email scooter@westpeek.ventures.

Common questions

Does it cost anything?

No. There is no membership fee and no paid tier.

How often will you email me?

Only when there is a real update: an event, a newsletter issue, or a podcast episode. There is no scheduled send.

Do I have to be a founder?

No. Operators, investors, creatives and builders are all part of it, and the mix is deliberate — the useful introductions usually cross roles.

Is this how I pitch the fund?

No, that is separate. West Peek Ventures takes submissions through its own pitch page.
