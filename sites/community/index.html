Origin

Started in 2020, during the global pandemic. West Peek began when 5,000 people virtually gathered on Sunday, March 22, 2020, to come together, learn, partner, and connect with one another. We saw then the power of community while building your dreams. West Peek is the continuation of that.

The Update · Quarterly

The Update

The Update is the lifeblood of West Peek — a safe space to share wins, big or small, ask for help, and talk through what's actually going on with what you're building. From what you share, we make the introductions, curate the workshops, and extend the dinner invites that fit where you are right now. We want you to feel less lonely as you build. It is not our independence but our interconnectedness that gets us there.

Pitch Competition

We host pitch competitions in partnership with Sengo, so you can get access to non-dilutive capital. This is a great way to practice your pitch — pitch in front of real investors to get real feedback, and in front of a live audience, which can lead to more opportunities and more awareness.

Prize: a $1,000 cash prize, along with a $500 Audience Choice Award.
