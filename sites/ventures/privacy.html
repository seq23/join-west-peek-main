Privacy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who we are

This site is run by West Peek Ventures. Questions or requests about your information: info@westpeek.ventures.

What you give us

When you fill in a form we receive what you type (such as your name, email, company and message) and any file you attach, such as a pitch deck.

Every form is emailed to our team so we can reply to you. The founder application, the pitch form and the newsletter signup also add you to the West Peek network list (a Google Sheet we keep) so we can stay in touch. We do not sell it.

To stop spam, our server counts how many forms each internet (IP) address sends in an hour. The count clears after the hour.

What we measure

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and buttons people use. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your browser’s details and an approximate location. We send it the page address with personal details removed from the link. Ad personalisation and Google signals are turned off. Google explains how it uses this information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a person, not a bot, is sending them: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

Who else handles it

Cloudflare hosts this site, checks our forms for bots and sends our form emails. Resend also sends our form emails. Google keeps the West Peek network list in Google Sheets. They handle it on our behalf.

Your choices

Email us to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to come off the network list. You can also block or clear cookies in your browser.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
