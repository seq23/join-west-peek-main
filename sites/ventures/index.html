What We Believe

Community creates competitive advantage in early-stage companies.

In the early stages of building a company, no matter the sector, the path to scale runs through people — your first believers, your earliest customers, the voices that carry your name before your brand can carry itself. Community is a unique point of leverage that compounds over time and stays with a company for years to come.

We're looking for founders who are uniquely building community within their industry — specifically in industries where trust matters most: healthcare, education, consumer, and the future of work.

Focus Sectors

Healthcare

Patient trust drives adoption. Community compounds clinical credibility.

Education

Learning is social. The best edtech is built on real relationships.

Consumer

Brand loyalty starts in community. Word-of-mouth beats paid acquisition.

Future of Work

As humans and AI work side by side, trust between people becomes the layer that holds teams, tools, and organizations together.
