About

Founder story

West Peek Productions began in 2020 as a virtual and hybrid event production partner, when organizations had to move their most important gatherings online almost overnight. It has produced more than 400 virtual and hybrid events since.

Scooter Taylor has spent his career building things people want to be part of: LookLive, QuarantineCon, Top Barz, and the wider West Peek community and ventures. The thread through all of it is the same: seeing overlooked potential in people, organizations and moments, and turning it into participation.

That is the edge. Plenty of agencies can find an editor or a designer. What we bring is judgment about what is worth making, and why an audience would respond to it.

Today the studio works across content and media, events and experiences, brand and storytelling, and web and digital, for organizations whose work deserves more attention than it is getting.

Production credibility

Live production teaches you to plan for everything.

Hundreds of live shows taught us to rehearse every speaker, plan the content before the event, and have an answer ready for every way a broadcast can fail. We bring the same discipline to every campaign, series and site.
