About

Our founding story.

West Peek Productions has produced more than 400 virtual and hybrid events. Once the world opened back up, it was clear people needed community more than ever - and organizations needed to understand how to build community deeper than virtual events.

One of the best ways to build community is digital content that educates, entertains, inspires, highlights, informs and empowers your audience. With a strong brand and consistency, you build a community online that leads to offline interactions aligned with your business goals.

This is our philosophy - we've done it and seen it several times.

The founder

Scooter Taylor.

Scooter Taylor, founder of West Peek Productions, has spent his career building community. In high school he built a YouTube channel of Apple enthusiasts to 20,000 subscribers, and went on to co-found companies like LookLive - backed by investors like Y Combinator, Comcast Ventures and Morgan Stanley. Scooter sees overlooked potential in people, organizations and moments, and turns it into compelling content that resonates.
