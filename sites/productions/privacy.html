Privacy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who we are

This site is run by West Peek Productions. Questions or requests about your information: info@westpeek.ventures.

What you give us

When you fill in the assessment form we receive what you type (your name, work email, organization and website) and your assessment answers and score.

It is emailed to West Peek Productions so we can reply about your project. It is not added to any mailing or network list, and we do not sell it.

To stop spam, our server counts how many forms each internet (IP) address sends in an hour. The count clears after the hour.

What we measure

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and buttons people use. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your browser’s details and an approximate location. We send it the page address with personal details removed from the link. Ad personalisation and Google signals are turned off. Google explains how it uses this information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

We also use Microsoft Clarity, which records how visitors move, click and scroll on our pages so we can find and fix problems. Clarity sets cookies. Microsoft’s privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement.

Who else handles it

Cloudflare hosts this site and sends our form emails. Resend also sends our form emails. They handle it on our behalf.

Your choices

Email us to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. You can also block or clear cookies in your browser.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
