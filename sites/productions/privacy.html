Privacy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who we are

This site is run by West Peek Productions. Questions or requests about your information: info@westpeek.ventures.

What you give us

When you fill in the Get in touch form we receive what you type: your name, work email, job title, organization and website, the services you are interested in, what you need, any event dates and how you heard about us. With it we receive the page you sent it from, the campaign tags in that page’s link (if any) and the name of the site that sent you here.

It is emailed to West Peek Productions and kept in our private client records so we can reply about your project. It is not added to any mailing or network list, and we do not sell it.

To stop spam, our server counts how many forms each internet (IP) address sends in an hour. The count clears after the hour.

What we measure

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and buttons people use. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your browser’s details and an approximate location. We send it the page address with personal details removed from the link, and never anything you type into a form. Ad personalisation and Google signals are turned off. Google explains how it uses this information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

We use no other analytics, advertising or session-recording tools on this site.

Who else handles it

Cloudflare hosts this site, stores our client records and sends our form emails. Resend also sends our form emails. They handle it on our behalf.

Your choices

Email us to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. You can also block or clear cookies in your browser.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
