Our story

We started with events. We learned the real opportunity was community.

West Peek Productions began in 2020 as a turnkey virtual and hybrid event production partner, helping organizations create seamless, high-quality experiences when connection had to rapidly move online.

After producing more than 400 virtual and hybrid events, one pattern became impossible to ignore: the event was rarely the whole job.

The organizations creating the strongest outcomes were building something around the moment—an audience, a relationship, a story, a brand, a network, or a community that continued after the screen went dark.

Today, West Peek Productions operates more broadly as a Community-as-a-Service and creative agency, helping organizations connect community strategy, experiences, storytelling, brand, content, and audience growth into one coherent engine.

We are backed by the broader West Peek community of 5,000+ founders, operators, investors, creatives, and builders—a network that gives us a firsthand understanding of what makes people gather, participate, collaborate, and stay connected.
