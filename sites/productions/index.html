About

Turnkey virtual and hybrid event production is our flagship. West Peek Productions delivers live experiences that are seamless, credible, and professionally run from start to finish. Our work spans conferences, summits, galas, career fairs, internal events, and other high-visibility moments where execution matters.

We bring real range and operating experience. We’ve produced more than 400 virtual and hybrid events across enterprise organizations, national nonprofits, and high-growth companies — supporting multi-session experiences, distributed audiences, and initiatives designed to reach at scale. Our approach is straightforward: clear planning, strong execution, and a production standard that holds up under pressure.

We do not treat events as isolated moments. In most cases, they’re part of a larger objective — whether that’s telling a story, reaching the right audience, or driving a specific outcome. We build with that in mind, and when needed, we support the content, messaging, podcast, and audience side of the work so the effort actually delivers — whether or not it starts with a live event.

Here’s how it works

Before

We start by getting clear on what success looks like — who the event needs to reach, what it needs to communicate, and how it should land.

During

We run the live experience in real time — managing speakers, flow, transitions, and technical execution so the event feels smooth and professional from start to finish, while ensuring the experience holds up in real time.

After

We make sure the work continues to create value. That can include recordings, usable content, and follow-through so the work continues to deliver value beyond the live moment.
