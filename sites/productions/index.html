About

West Peek Productions specializes in turnkey virtual and hybrid event production — delivering live experiences that are seamless, credible, and professionally run from start to finish. Our work spans conferences, summits, galas, career fairs, internal events, and other high-visibility moments where execution matters.

We’ve produced more than 400 virtual and hybrid events across enterprise organizations, national nonprofits, and high-growth companies — supporting multi-session experiences, distributed audiences, and initiatives designed to reach at scale. Our approach is straightforward: clear planning, strong execution, and a production standard that holds up under pressure.

We don’t treat events as isolated moments. In most cases, they’re part of a larger objective — whether that’s telling a story, reaching the right audience, or driving a specific outcome. We build with that in mind, and when needed, we support the content, messaging, and audience side of the work so the effort actually delivers — whether or not it starts with a live event.

Here’s how it works

Before

During
