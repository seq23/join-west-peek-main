Audience growth

We help organisations think beyond impressions toward repeat engagement, distribution, participation and sustained growth.

The short answer

Audience strategy, distribution, partnerships, programming, amplification and community-led growth.

The framing we start from: an impression is someone who saw you once. An audience is someone who comes back. Those require different work, and most growth budgets are spent on the first while the goal is the second.

What we work on

  • Audience strategyWho you are actually trying to reach repeatedly, which is usually a narrower group than the one in the current plan.
  • DistributionWhere the work goes after it is made. Chronically under-resourced relative to production, and usually the cheapest available improvement.
  • PartnershipsBorrowing the trust of an audience someone else has already earned.
  • ProgrammingA reason to return on a rhythm, rather than a reason to notice once.
  • AmplificationConcentrating attention on the things worth concentrating it on.
  • Community-led growthThe version where your audience brings the next one, which is the only kind that compounds without proportional spend.

The cycle we design for

Experience creates content. Content grows audience. Audience strengthens community. Community creates the next opportunity. One effort should create more than one outcome, and audience growth is the stage where that stops being a slogan and starts being a distribution plan.
