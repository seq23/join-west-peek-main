West Peek Productions/Services

What we do

Community, content and experiences that connect people and move organisations forward — under one operating partner rather than five disconnected vendors.

The short answer

West Peek Productions is a Community-as-a-Service and creative agency. We produce virtual and hybrid events, live streams, webinars and executive broadcasts, and we build the community strategy, storytelling, brand and audience growth that make those moments compound.

We have produced more than 400 virtual and hybrid experiences since 2020. Production pricing starts in a published band of $2,500 to $7,500 for moderated sessions.

Production

The live work: the shows, the streams and the broadcasts.

  • Virtual & hybrid event productionConferences, summits, galas and career fairs, produced end to end.
  • Live stream productionSingle-camera to multi-source broadcasts, with a planned failover.
  • Webinar productionModerated sessions and series, in the published $2,500 to $7,500 band.
  • Executive broadcastsAll-hands and leadership moments where the stakes are internal.

Strategy and creative

The work that decides whether a production was worth making, and whether anything survives it.

  • Community strategy & activationWho the community is for, why people participate, what keeps them.
  • Storytelling & contentTurning experiences into assets that keep working afterwards.
  • Brand & creativePositioning, messaging, creative direction and launch creative.
  • Audience growthBeyond impressions: repeat engagement, distribution, participation.

Smarter operations

We also streamline the repetitive workflows and connect the systems that keep community and creative programmes moving. Where useful this can include selective AI-enabled automation, used as infrastructure rather than as the headline.

How the work compounds

An event can create content. Content can grow an audience. An audience can strengthen a community. A strong community creates the next opportunity. We design with that full cycle in mind, which is the reason these services sit together rather than in separate agencies.
