Client: NCTA Education FoundationProject: The Ripple EffectWhat happens after the big event?

The problem

What happens after the big event? That was the question the NCTA Education Foundation kept asking themselves. For years they had produced the Kaitz Event in New York and energized their community but afterwards people went back to their normal lives and not much attention was on the organization.

What we did

We first took time to understand what was really happening at the Kaitz Event. They were awarding grants to organizations so that they could continue making impact. Ripples so to speak. Thus we created “The Ripple Effect” as a podcast series. We handled the production end-to-end and made things very turnkey for the client. We developed the branding, logo and social media strategy for the podcast. We handled the filming and editing of each episode. We were able to film the season over a 1.5 week period handling all speaker logistics for virtual and in-person guests. Our social strategy involved coordinating with the guests and their teams to ensure we had the social media rollout for each episode was coordinated for maximum visibility.

Results

A weekly social media plan for the marketing team to follow, and a six-episode podcast which resulted in 30 pieces of content for the marketing team to post and keep their audience engaged. The content is posted across YouTube, LinkedIn and Instagram.

Client: Executive Leadership CouncilProject: 2026 Mid-Level Managers’ SymposiumHow could we alleviate the work of producing two conferences for one team.

The problem

The client hosted their symposium in D.C., but many of their members were attending virtually and needed an engaging experience over the two days. How could we alleviate the work of producing two conferences for one team.

What we did

We handled the technology, production and support for the virtual experience. We managed the technology platform which handled a custom design to match the client’s branding, concurrent breakout sessions, main stages, chats, polls and sponsor expo booths. We produced the event according to the separate virtual run of show complete with a host and then we made sure every speaker was properly sound checked; we tested their audio, video, wifi, background setup and then on the day of handled logistics with call times and calendar invites.

Results

A successful 2-day conference with hundreds of attendees. No interruptions and able to adjust on the fly to various updates from the in-person experience.

Client: Foundation for The Mid-SouthProject: Webinar SeriesHow do you engage this audience?

The problem

A foundation which produces strategic reports, data and creates impact with partners across the Delta region of The United States. The content was not reaching a wide audience with very low engagement on LinkedIn even though they had 2,000 followers. How do you engage this audience?

What we did

We produced monthly webinars that streamed lived to their LinkedIn audience. We created branding and production graphics to match the Foundation’s branding. We streamed the conversations live to their LinkedIn Page and Facebook Page, and created social media clips from the webinar’s most impactful moments. We also hosted pre-production meetings where speakers were able to work on their presentation. Each webinar had it’s own theme and design

Results

Two quarters of successful webinars that were very turnkey for the small team of three. The webinars and social clips still pull in views months after the webinar which helps keep the Foundation’s work top of mind.

Client: TNTPProject: Strategic Plan Social PlanHow do you take an 80 page strategic plan and create social content and a calendar that can engage the current Instagram and reach new audiences?

The problem

On the precipice of announcing a major strategic plan, the marketing team needed to extend their bench for this special campaign really understanding the nuances of Instagram. How do you take an 80 page strategic plan and create social content and a calendar that can engage the current Instagram and reach new audiences?

What we did

First, we read the strategic plan. We had to understand the material inside and out and find key moments that could translate well on social media. We took these key points from the plan, and mapped them to formats that work on well on social media: Carousels, Videos, Street Interviews and more. We understood that social media today requires having an authentic voice talking consistently. So we built a series surrounding their CEO which ignited her LinkedIn connections causing interest in the strategic plan.

Results

Over 20 pieces of content in an 8 week span. We saw an audience growth TNTP’s Instagram audience reach increased approximately 327%, total views rose 130%, and net follower additions grew 32% compared with the pre-campaign period.
